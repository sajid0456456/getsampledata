    Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy explains how GetSampleData.com ("we," "us," or "the Site") handles information when you visit and use our website. We built this Site to be as simple and low-footprint as possible, and that extends to how we handle your data.

    Information We Collect: GetSampleData.com does not require account registration, and you do not need to provide any personal information to browse categories or download sample files. If you use our Contact page, we collect only what you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and message content, in order to respond to you.

    Automatically Collected Information: Like most websites, our hosting provider and any third-party form or analytics tools we may use can automatically log standard technical details such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This information is used only in aggregate, to understand how the Site is used and to keep it running reliably.

    Cookies: The Site does not use cookies to track you across other websites. Any cookies or local storage used are limited to basic functionality, such as remembering your language preference.

    How We Use Information: Any information submitted through the Contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry, resolve issues with a sample file, or consider suggestions for new formats. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing purposes.

    Sharing of Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers strictly to operate the Site, such as a contact-form processor or hosting service, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform that function.

    Children's Privacy: GetSampleData.com is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    Your Choices: If you've contacted us and would like your message or information removed from our records, reach out through the Contact page and we'll take care of it.

    Changes to This Policy: We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version published on this page is always the current one in effect.

    Contact: If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
