Cooldown backtest

Simulate a per-coin re-entry cooldown. After each trade you'd actually take closes, that same coin is blocked from opening a new trade for the next X hours — so if a later trade on that coin entered before the cooldown expired, it's skipped entirely, as if the cooldown had been running live the whole time. Runs against the trades currently selected by the entry-date filter below.

Filter by entry date

Each button is a calendar day found in the Timestamp (entry time) column. All days are selected by default — click a day to toggle it off/on. Only trades entered on the selected day(s) feed every table, filter, and simulation below. The badge on each day shows its total trade count; once you run a simulation it switches to kept / total for that day, reflecting your active Entry Filters.

Optional: narrow every selected day down to the same time-of-day window (e.g. 09:00–14:00). Leave a field blank to use the full day. One shared window applies across your whole selection above.
